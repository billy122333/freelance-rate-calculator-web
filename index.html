FAQ

Freelance pricing questions this tool answers

How do I calculate my freelance hourly rate?

A sustainable freelance hourly rate usually starts with your monthly take-home goal, realistic billable hours, business overhead, and a margin for tax and uncertainty.

How do I quote a freelance project?

Estimate project hours first, then apply a rate that already includes your cost structure. If the scope may expand, raise the revision or risk buffer before sending the quote.

Why not use average market rates only?

Market averages can be useful context, but they do not know your billable capacity, expenses, or profit needs. This calculator is meant to give you a defensible internal floor.
